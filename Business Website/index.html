The Process.

A glimpse behind the seams. From initial sketching and pattern drafting to the final meticulous hand-stitching that brings our haute couture visions to life.

View Full Process

01. Conceptualization

Every piece begins as a vision on paper, sketching silhouettes and exploring textile possibilities.

02. Textile Sourcing

Curating the finest sustainable materials, from organic silks to deadstock structured wools.

03. Pattern Drafting

Translating 2D sketches into complex 3D mathematics, ensuring the garment flows perfectly on the body.

04. Draping

Molding the fabric directly onto the dress form to capture natural movement and organic volume.

05. Construction

Precision machine sewing binds the structural foundation of the garment together with invisible seams.

06. Hand Finishing

Hours of meticulous hand-stitching, applying lace appliqués and beadwork for the final haute couture touch.

Campaigns.

Watch the garments come alive in motion. Our cinematic ad campaigns capture the ethereal movement of haute couture against stark realities.

Midnight Solstice

Watch our latest cinematic campaign unfold. This visual editorial captures the ethereal movement of haute couture against stark, industrial environments, emphasizing the contrast between delicate craftsmanship and modern cityscapes.

The Concept

Shot entirely on 4K digital cinema cameras at midnight, the campaign explores the intersection of light and shadow. We aimed to showcase how silk organza and detailed hand-beading catch ambient city lights, creating a natural luminescent effect without relying on studio lighting.

The Garment

The featured dress is a masterclass in structural fluid dynamics. It required over 120 hours of intricate hand-pleating to achieve its signature liquid-metal drape. The asymmetric hem line provides an unrestricted range of motion, allowing the model's stride to breathe life into the silhouette.
